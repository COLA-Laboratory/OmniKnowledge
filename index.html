Questions.

What do I need to start?

A research question and a small set of seed papers. You choose the search steps and the criteria every paper must meet.

How is the cost kept down?

Quick checks such as year, citations, venue and keywords run first. A language model then reads only the titles and abstracts that are left.

Can I check the result?

Every search reports the papers it found and every filter the papers it removed. Every screening decision keeps its reason, and every statement in an output leads back to the papers and passages it rests on.
