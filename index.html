Our approach

Comprehensive

Triangulated search combines citations, semantics and keywords to deliver the most complete picture of your field.

Rigorous

Exact metadata rules and LLM screening, from title to full text, hold every paper to your criteria.

Cost-effective

Cascaded screening lets cheap rules settle the clear cases and saves LLM screening for the hard ones.

Trustworthy

Transparent search and screening pipelines, with a fully auditable record of every decision, keep you in control.

Triangulated search across citations, semantics and keywords

Three search paradigms run as one and take the best of each. Citation tracing follows how papers build on one another, semantic search finds related work whatever its wording, and database search reaches papers no citation leads to.

Screening that scales within your budget

Rule checks on year, citations, venue and keywords cost almost nothing. They lean toward keeping a paper, since a wrong rejection cannot be undone, and leave the doubtful ones to LLM screening of titles, then abstracts, then full texts.

Pipelines you can build, monitor and audit

A platform for knowledge retrieval pipelines. Every search reports the papers it found and every filter the papers it passes on, so a run can be checked from end to end.

Search your way

Chain backward and forward citation tracing, semantic search and database search in any order, or run them side by side.

Set your own bar

Choose from a library of criteria, from a publication year to an LLM judgment on the full text.

A mapped knowledge corpus, ready to power your research

The papers that pass stand as a citation network or by topic. For a literature review, a hypothesis, an experiment plan or a reading of your results, every paper is read and each statement cites its source.

Questions

What is OmniKnowledge?

A web app for systematic literature work. Give it a research topic and a few seed papers; it searches, screens and maps the literature into a corpus you can review, question and build on.

Where do the papers come from?

From a scholarly index of over 200 million papers across every field: journal articles, conference proceedings and preprints, including those on PubMed, arXiv and bioRxiv.

Can I trust the results?

Every step is open: each search lists what it found, each filter what it removed and why, with PRISMA-style counts throughout. It is not another LLM agent: an LLM screens only the borderline papers that rules cannot settle, which keeps both the cost and the room for hallucination small.

What does a run cost?

About $0.50 for a typical run that screens 20,000 papers. Rule-based filters remove most papers at no cost, so an LLM screens only the few that rules cannot settle. The cost grows with scope and settings.

How can I get access?

OmniKnowledge is in internal testing. Access will open first to collaborating groups, then by request. Request access to join the list; meanwhile, the demo walks through a complete run.
